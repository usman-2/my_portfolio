FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Social media management questions.

How much does monthly social media management cost?+
Monthly management is custom quoted according to the number of reels, posts, posters, ad creatives and any scheduling or other tasks. Standard single reel edits cost PKR 1,000 and standalone thumbnails PKR 400. Eligible 30-day recurring plans cost PKR 500 per reel and PKR 200 per thumbnail, with the total based on volume.
Does the 30-day plan cost PKR 500 in total?+
No. PKR 500 is the discounted price for each standard edited reel within an agreed 30-day package; PKR 200 is the discounted per-thumbnail price. We agree on the number of deliverables and the total cost before work starts.
Can you make promotional posters and advertisements?+
Yes, branded offer posters, story graphics, social media ad creatives and simple video ad edits can be included. Paid advertising spend and campaign management are separate, if requested.
Do you guarantee followers, views or sales?+
No. These services cover agreed content production and support, not guaranteed reach, platform approval, sales or engagement.
